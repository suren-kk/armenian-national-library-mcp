# Rights, Takedown, and Correction Requests

Effective date: 2026-07-14

Contact: Suren Karapetyan, `surenakar@gmail.com`

`armenian-national-library-mcp` is independent software and does not host the authoritative NLA repository. Nevertheless, the maintainer will review good-faith reports that the project's code, documentation, fixtures, mappings, or project-controlled distribution improperly includes or facilitates access to protected or inaccurate material.

## Submit a request

Email `surenakar@gmail.com` with the subject `armenian-national-library-mcp rights request` and include:

- your name and a reliable contact method;
- whether you are the rightsholder, data subject, authorized representative, or another reporter;
- the exact repository path, release artifact, NLA item handle, bitstream UUID, or URL at issue;
- a description of the protected work, personal information, or inaccurate record;
- the right or concern asserted and the territory, if relevant;
- the requested action and why it is appropriate; and
- a good-faith statement that the information supplied is accurate.

Do not send identity documents, unrelated personal data, full copyrighted works, or sensitive evidence unless the maintainer requests a secure method. Knowingly false claims may harm users and rightsholders.

## Review process

The maintainer will:

1. acknowledge a sufficiently detailed request within seven calendar days when practicable;
2. identify whether the issue is in project-controlled material, an upstream NLA record, a downstream provider, or a self-hosted deployment;
3. preserve the minimum evidence needed to review the request;
4. remove, disable, correct, or qualify project-controlled material when appropriate;
5. refer source-record concerns to the NLA and deployment-specific concerns to the relevant operator;
6. notify materially affected contributors or users when appropriate and lawful; and
7. document the decision and reconsider it if reliable counter-evidence is supplied.

Urgent reports involving exposed credentials, immediate physical danger, or highly sensitive personal data should say `URGENT` in the subject. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Limits

The maintainer cannot edit or remove an NLA-hosted record or file, an npm/GitHub copy outside the maintainer's control, an AI provider's retained data, or an independently self-hosted deployment. A project patch or new release does not automatically update existing installations; operators must upgrade and clear any relevant caches.

This process is a voluntary project policy. It does not waive legal rights, admit liability, replace a legally required notice, or guarantee a particular outcome.
