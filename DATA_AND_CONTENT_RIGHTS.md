## How the software accesses material

The software performs user-initiated, read-only requests to the configured public NLA DSpace API. It does not include NLA records or documents in the source distribution, bypass access controls, authenticate to NLA, upload material, or modify the repository. It can relay metadata, bounded repository-provided text, and canonical NLA links to the user's MCP client. Runtime caches are in memory and temporary.

No API-specific licence or automated-access terms were located on the official public NLA/DSpace surfaces during the project's 2026-07-14 review. This statement is not proof that no other policy or agreement exists. Users and self-hosting operators must review the terms and rights that apply to their use and must stop or narrow access if requested by the NLA or a rightsholder.

The NLA's published [digitization rules](https://nla.am/en_US/digitization) describe restrictions on copying copyrighted books and articles and prohibit copying some categories of material. The [NLA Library Rules](https://nla.am/public/en_US/library-rules) also place responsibility for use of electronic resources on the user. These pages are informative references; this project does not interpret them as an API licence or as permission for any particular reuse.
